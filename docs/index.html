1. Information We Collect

Flising Ride (Passenger) and Flising Driver collect the following data strictly to provide ride-hailing and parcel delivery services in Papua New Guinea:

  • Account & Profile Data: Name, phone number, email address, profile photo, and driver verification documents (driver's license and vehicle registration).
